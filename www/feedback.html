Maintenance and releases

denglab maintains AvianViromeDB and releases corrections and additions as needed. Each release is archived separately on Zenodo, and earlier versions remain citable.

Release notes will identify the date, data scope, and changes affecting identifiers or annotations. Cite the version DOI of the release used in your work.
